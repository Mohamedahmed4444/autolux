About
AutoLux

14 years of passion, trust, and excellence in automotive retail.

Since 2010

Built on a Passion
for the Road

AutoLux was founded in 2010 with a simple yet powerful vision: to make the process of buying a premium car in Egypt transparent, enjoyable, and fair. What started as a small showroom in Heliopolis with 12 cars has grown into Egypt's most respected automotive destination, hosting over 200 vehicles from 15+ world-class brands.

We believe every driver deserves a car that fits their life — and every buyer deserves honesty. That's why we've built our reputation on trust, competitive pricing, and genuine customer care long after the sale is complete.
